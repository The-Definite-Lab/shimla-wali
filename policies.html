Refund & Returns Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Fair & Transparent Policy

1. Farm-to-Table Commitment

At Shimla Crunch, we take profound pride in dispatching freshly harvested, tree-ripened Himalayan produce. Every order is packed with cellular foam cushioning and individually hand-inspected by 4th-generation orchard growers in Kotkhai before dispatch.

Nature of Perishable Goods

Fresh apples, hand-cracked walnuts, and natural orchard harvest are perishable commodities. To maintain cold-chain integrity and prevent health risks, we do not require or accept physical return shipments of opened or spoiled fruits back to Himachal Pradesh.

2. What To Do In Case of Transit Damage or Spoilage

While our cellular protective packaging absorbs road vibration, rare courier handling incidents may occur. If you receive fruit that has suffered transit bruising or spoilage, we resolve it promptly:

  1. Record an Unboxing Video: Kindly record a quick unboxing video or take clear photos when opening your carton or pouch.
  2. Notify Us Within 24 Hours: Inform our Orchard Care team within 24 hours of package delivery so our batch dispatch records can trace the incident.
  3. Send to WhatsApp / Email: Share your Order ID and photo/video proof via WhatsApp at +91 8580861606 or email care@shimlacrunch.com.

3. Resolution Options (Full / Partial Refund or Replacement)

  • Pro-Rata / Full Refund: Once verified by our harvest coordinator, we immediately issue a partial or 100% full refund proportional to the affected fruit quantity.
  • Free Replacement Carton: If severe box crushing occurs during courier transit, an immediate fresh replacement box will be express-dispatched from Kotkhai at zero extra cost.
  • Processing Time: Approved refunds are credited directly to your original payment method (UPI, Bank Account, or Card) within 5 to 7 business days.
Important Exclusions

Refunds or replacements are applicable strictly for genuine physical damage, transit spoilage, or incorrect items delivered. Natural biological variations in fruit color, light surface russeting (natural sun marks), or minor size variance are inherent to authentic unwaxed mountain farming and do not qualify for returns.

Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Express Air & Surface Dispatch

1. Direct Orchard Dispatch from Kotkhai (7,500 Ft)

Unlike commercial supermarket brands that store apples in industrial cold storage warehouses for 6 to 9 months, Shimla Crunch picks and packs your fruit directly in our Kotkhai orchards within hours of your order confirmation.

2. Cellular Cushion Packaging

High-altitude mountain transit requires zero-bruise engineering. We pack our harvest in specialized cellular foam trays inside heavy-duty, five-ply ventilated corrugated master cartons to guarantee safe arrival.

3. Estimated Transit Times Across India

  • Delhi-NCR & North India: 2 to 3 Business Days from dispatch.
  • Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad & Western India: 3 to 4 Business Days from dispatch.
  • Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Chennai & South India: 3 to 5 Business Days from dispatch.
  • Kolkata, Guwahati & Eastern India: 4 to 6 Business Days from dispatch.

4. Real-Time WhatsApp Tracking

As soon as our courier partner scans your carton at the Shimla sorting facility, an automated WhatsApp message and SMS containing your live tracking URL and courier AWB number is sent to your mobile number.

5. Delivery Attempts & Address Accuracy

Our courier partners make up to 3 delivery attempts. Please ensure your mobile number and shipping address (including landmark and correct PIN code) are accurate during checkout to prevent unnecessary delays.
