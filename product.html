Questions, Answered

Everything You'd Like To Know

Still curious? Our ghee care team is always here to assist your wellness journey.

What makes Bilona Ghee different?
+
Traditional Bilona Ghee begins with cultured curd, which is churned with a wooden bilona to separate fresh butter and then slowly heated over low fire. This patient method gives the ghee its distinctive nutty aroma, granular texture, and superior bio-availability.
Is Shimla Wali ghee made only from indigenous (Desi) cows?
+
Yes, absolutely. We source our milk exclusively from purebred, pasture-grazed Gir cows in Himachal Pradesh.
Why does the texture change with the season?
+
Pure ghee melts in summer and solidifies in winter. The famous 'danedar' (granular) texture is a natural property of A2 Bilona ghee, proving it has not been homogenized or artificially stabilized.
Does it contain preservatives or artificial colors?
+
Zero. Shimla Wali Ghee is 100% pure and natural, free from any preservatives, colors, or chemical additives.
