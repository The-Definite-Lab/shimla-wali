Questions, Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about our harvest, packaging, or pan-India delivery? We are here to help.

How do you protect fresh apples during pan-India courier?
+
Every single apple is hand-graded and nestled inside custom shock-absorbent foam honeycomb trays within heavy-duty corrugated cartons. Our packaging has a 99.4% damage-free delivery rate across metro and non-metro cities.
Are Kinnauri apples waxed or treated?
+
Zero wax, zero chemicals. Commercial supermarket apples are polished with artificial wax to look shiny. Our apples carry their natural mountain bloom, allowing you to eat them unpeeled with complete peace of mind.
What makes Kinnauri walnuts 'Kagzi' (paper-shell)?
+
Kinnauri Kagzi walnuts possess exceptionally thin shells that can easily be cracked between two palms or with bare fingers without needing a nutcracker. They are high in healthy natural oils, non-bitter, and completely unbleached.
