WHO WE ARE

The Story of Shimla Crunch

Nestled under the famous HATU PEAK lies the Kalbog-Baghi valley, one of the premier high-altitude apple-producing belts of Himachal Pradesh. Operated by the Khalain family, our farming legacy stretches back across four generations of passionate apple growers.

Shimla Crunch, a brainchild of Prajwal and Kushan, came into existence only after years of offering our produce to friends and getting the exact same reply: "This apple tastes completely different from what we buy in city retail markets."

Pondering over this, we researched the commercial market only to find that traditional resellers push the shelf life of fruit to the absolute limit—frequently selling produce that has been locked in artificial cold-storage warehouses for months.

Our mission was clear: Cut out every middleman and ship tree-ripened, unwaxed fruits directly from the orchard branches to consumers' homes across India.

Direct-to-Consumer Model

By cutting out wholesale mandis and commission agents, we retain 100% control over quality, ensuring you receive fruit harvested days, not months, ago.

European High-Density Grafting

We pioneered imported European planting material in Kotkhai. This modern method allows higher density planting and results in exceptionally sweet, juicy apples.

Signature Fruit Crunch

Our low-temperature dehydration process crafts addictive, healthy apple crisps without oil, frying, or added sugar—the pure crunch of Himachal.
