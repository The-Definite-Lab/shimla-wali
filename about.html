Pure • Local • Natural • The Soul of Himachal Pradesh

THE SHIMLA WALI HERITAGE

Rooted In The High Himalayas

At Shimla Wali, our journey started with a heartfelt purpose: to connect discerning families across India directly with the untouched, pristine purity of Himachal Pradesh.

We are not an industrial factory or a commercial warehouse. We are a direct trust with small Himachali households, farmers, and orchard growers across Shimla, Kinnaur, and Chamba. From pure A2 Desi Ghee hand-churned in wooden Bilona, to thin-shelled Kagzi Walnuts, raw multiflora forest honey, grandmother's sun-cured pickles, crisp mountain apples, and mineral-rich pulses — everything is 100% natural and harvested with love.

High-Altitude Pastures

Our indigenous cows graze freely across Himalayan hillsides rich in wild medicinal herbs, clover, and snow-melt water.

Traditional Vedic Bilona

Cultured curd butter is gently churned by hand with a two-way wooden churner and slow-simmered in clay and brass vessels.

Zero Chemical Processing

No chemical bleaching, no artificial wax on apples, no high-heat pasteurization of honey, and no artificial food colors.
